# Fires

Fire comes up again and again in this project. Grand buildings burn soon after they open, burn again decades later, or are gutted and rebuilt inside their old walls. In the alternative view, fire is one of the main ways old buildings were cleared away or "rebuilt" (see the [fire and demolition](ALTERNATIVE-VIEW.md#the-main-ideas) theme). The ordinary explanation is that 19th-century buildings, full of timber, gas lighting, stage scenery and exhibits, were genuinely at risk.

This page lists every building in the project that has been damaged or destroyed by fire, and, separately, those damaged by bombing in war. Click a building to go to its entry.

The lists are generated automatically by `scripts/build_index.py` from the **Fires** and **War damage** rows in each entry's details table, so don't edit them by hand. To add a building, give its entry a row like this, with each event separated by a semicolon:

```markdown
| **Fires** | 9 June 1873: destroyed 16 days after opening; 10 July 1980: about a third destroyed |
```

## What to look for

For each fire, the entries try to answer the same questions:

- **When**, and how soon after the building was finished?
- **The cause**: was it established, and was there an inquiry or inquest?
- **What was lost**, and what survived (walls, towers, foundations)?
- **What happened next**: was the building rebuilt, and to the same design, or cleared away?
