# The alternative view

This page explains the ideas behind the "alternative view" sections in this project: where they come from, what they say, how they apply to the buildings here, and who to watch and read to go deeper.

## Where the ideas come from

- **Tartary on old maps.** From the 1500s to the 1800s, many European maps labelled a huge area of northern and central Asia as *Tartaria* or *Great Tartary*. In mainstream history this was a loose name for lands Europeans knew little about, inhabited by Tatar, Mongol and Turkic peoples. The alternative view reads it as evidence of a forgotten empire.
- **New Chronology (1970s–1980s onwards).** Russian mathematician Anatoly Fomenko and his co-author Gleb Nosovsky argued that accepted world history is badly mis-dated and that much of it was compressed or invented. Many later "Tartaria" ideas build on this.
- **Nikolai Levashov.** A Russian writer who popularised the idea of a "Great Tartaria" as a lost civilisation in the 2000s.
- **The English-speaking internet (about 2016–2018 onwards).** The ideas spread through Reddit forums and YouTube channels, combining Tartaria with "mud flood" theories and a focus on grand 19th-century buildings.

## The main ideas

| Idea | What it claims | Examples in this project |
|---|---|---|
| **An older civilisation (Tartaria)** | Many grand buildings were inherited from an advanced worldwide civilisation and later claimed or re-dated | [Villa Sultana](places/italy/villa-sultana/villa-sultana.md), [Saint Isaac's Cathedral](places/russia/saint-isaacs-cathedral/saint-isaacs-cathedral.md) |
| **Mud flood** | A catastrophe buried city ground floors in mud, which is why windows and doors are found below street level | [Seattle Underground](places/usa/seattle-underground/seattle-underground.md), [St Pancras undercroft](places/uk/st-pancras/st-pancras.md) |
| **The reset** | A break in history around 1800–1900 erased knowledge, with records lost and cities "rebuilt" | [Palace of Westminster](places/uk/palace-of-westminster/palace-of-westminster.md) (records burned in 1834) |
| **World's fairs** | Exhibitions showcased existing buildings as new, then removed them by demolition or fire | [Crystal Palace](places/uk/crystal-palace/crystal-palace.md), [Palace of Fine Arts](places/usa/palace-of-fine-arts-chicago/palace-of-fine-arts-chicago.md), [Palais du Trocadéro](places/france/palais-du-trocadero/palais-du-trocadero.md) |
| **Fire and demolition** | Grand buildings were destroyed soon after "completion" to erase them | [Anhalter Bahnhof](places/germany/anhalter-bahnhof/anhalter-bahnhof.md), [Crystal Palace](places/uk/crystal-palace/crystal-palace.md) |
| **Lost energy technology** | Domes, spires, copper and mercury were part of a free-energy system | [Frankfurt Hauptbahnhof](places/germany/frankfurt-hauptbahnhof/frankfurt-hauptbahnhof.md), [Rocchetta Mattei](places/italy/rocchetta-mattei/rocchetta-mattei.md) |
| **Impossible timelines** | Buildings were too big, too fast or too precise for their stated dates | [Crystal Palace](places/uk/crystal-palace/crystal-palace.md), [Cologne Cathedral](places/germany/cologne-cathedral/cologne-cathedral.md) |
| **Star forts** | The same geometry worldwide suggests a common origin or a non-military purpose | [Palmanova](places/italy/palmanova/palmanova.md), [Goryōkaku](places/japan/goryokaku/goryokaku.md) |
| **Shared architecture** | The same domes and styles appear on every continent at once | See the [glossary](GLOSSARY.md), for example Dome and Onion dome |

## People and places to watch

A starting list for exploring the alternative view. The project doesn't endorse every claim these sources make: use them for leads and arguments, then check each claim against the evidence and label it (see [How to weigh what you watch](#how-to-weigh-what-you-watch)).

### Alternative history researchers

| Who | Where | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Jon Levi** | YouTube | Architectural anomalies and "hidden history"; one of the channels that brought Tartaria to English-speaking audiences |
| **Martin Liedtke** | YouTube | Mud flood and Tartaria videos |
| **Sylvie Ivanowa** | YouTube | Architectural anomalies |
| **Stolen History** | stolenhistory.net | Community site collecting old photos, maps and theories |
| **r/Tartaria** and **r/CulturalLayer** | Reddit | Forums where the ideas spread, with many old photos and maps |

### Founding books and thinkers

| Who | What | Why read it |
|---|---|---|
| **Anatoly Fomenko and Gleb Nosovsky** | *New Chronology* series (English editions include *History: Fiction or Science?*) | The original argument that accepted dating is wrong |
| **Nikolai Levashov** | Books on "Great Tartaria" (mostly in Russian) | Where the Tartaria-as-civilisation idea was popularised |
| **Heribert Illig** | The "phantom time" hypothesis (1991) | Argues that about 300 years of the early Middle Ages never happened; related to the "reset" and dating questions |

## How to weigh what you watch

- **Note the claim, then find the source.** If a video says a building went up in a year, look for the records: newspapers, contracts, photos.
- **Label it.** Use the project's evidence labels: **Documented**, **Observed**, **Reported** or **Speculative**.
- **Look for the ordinary explanation too.** For example, the "Ouverture 1911" caption on [Villa Sultana](places/italy/villa-sultana/villa-sultana.md) might mean the start of a casino season. Testing both readings is what makes a finding stand up.
- **Bring leads back here.** Add them to an entry's **Research notes** section, or to the [research list](links/research-list.md), so they can be followed up.
